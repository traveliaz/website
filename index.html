Questions about solo travel in Egypt

Is Egypt safe for solo travelers?

Most solo travelers visit Egypt without problems. Stay in well-reviewed central areas, use registered taxis or ride-hailing apps, and book guided experiences for remote sites. Every hostel we list shows its guest rating and number of reviews, so you can see how other travelers rated it.

How does Traveliaz pick recommended hostels?

We pull listings and guest ratings from Booking.com, Expedia, Hostelworld and other partners, and pick the best-rated properties in each city, weighting places with many reviews from solo travelers.

Do I pay more by booking through Traveliaz?

No. Stays are booked directly on the partner platform at its listed price. We show the lowest available rate side by side.

Which Egyptian cities do your tours cover?

Cairo, Alexandria, Hurghada, Sharm El Sheikh, Luxor and Aswan, with licensed guides and small-group or private options.

Can travel agencies and exhibitors work with Traveliaz?

Yes. Our business desk bundles recommended hotels, flights and car rentals for agencies, corporate groups and trade-exhibition delegations.
