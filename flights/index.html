Egypt's main airports

Fly straight into the region you want to see and save a day of ground travel.

CAI

Cairo International

Egypt's main hub, for Cairo, Giza and the pyramids. Most long-haul flights land here.

SPX

Sphinx International

West of Cairo, close to Giza and the Grand Egyptian Museum.

HBE

Borg El Arab

The airport for Alexandria and the Mediterranean coast.

LXR

Luxor International

For the Valley of the Kings, Karnak and Nile cruises.

ASW

Aswan International

For Philae, Nubian villages and day trips to Abu Simbel.

HRG

Hurghada International

Red Sea beaches, diving and snorkeling, with many direct flights from Europe.

SSH

Sharm El Sheikh International

South Sinai, Ras Mohammed and trips to Mount Sinai.

RMF

Marsa Alam International

The quieter southern Red Sea coast and its reefs.

Questions about flights to Egypt

How does the Traveliaz flight search work?

The search compares fares from airlines and online travel agencies in one place. When you pick a fare you book directly with that airline or agency at the price it shows, and they issue your ticket.

Does Traveliaz add a fee to flight prices?

No. You pay the seller's own price. Traveliaz may earn a commission from the seller at no extra cost to you.

Which airports in Egypt can I fly into?

The main international airports are Cairo (CAI), Sphinx near Giza (SPX), Hurghada (HRG), Sharm El Sheikh (SSH), Luxor (LXR), Aswan (ASW), Marsa Alam (RMF) and Borg El Arab for Alexandria (HBE).

Can I search hotels together with flights?

Yes. Hotel offers for your destination appear next to the flight results, and our hostel picks for solo travelers are on the Traveliaz home page.
