Cairo travel guide: hostels, Pyramids tours & things to do

Cairo is where most trips to Egypt begin: the Pyramids of Giza, the Grand Egyptian Museum, Islamic Cairo's mosques and the Khan el-Khalili bazaar, all in one huge, loud, friendly city on the Nile.

Below are the highest-rated hostels in Cairo with their real Hostelworld scores, tours we run with licensed local guides, and the practical basics for getting in and around.

AirportCairo International (CAI), about 20 km from downtown. Sphinx International (SPX) is closer to Giza.
Best time to visitOctober to April, when days are warm rather than hot.
How long to stay3 to 4 days for the Pyramids, the museums and Islamic Cairo.
Getting aroundRide-hailing apps such as Uber and Careem, and the Cairo Metro for downtown.

Where to stay in Cairo

Downtown (around Tahrir)

The cheapest beds and the easiest metro access. Walk to the Egyptian Museum and the Nile corniche.

Zamalek

A leafy island neighbourhood with cafés and a calmer feel, a short ride from downtown.

Giza (near the Pyramids)

Wake up with a view of the Pyramids and walk to the plateau. Further from the rest of the city.

Where should solo travelers stay in Cairo?

Downtown near Tahrir Square is the most popular base: it has the most hostels, the metro and the Egyptian Museum on foot. Choose Giza if the Pyramids are your main focus.

How do I get from Cairo airport to downtown?

Ride-hailing apps work from Cairo International Airport, or you can prebook a private transfer at a fixed price. The drive takes about 40 to 60 minutes depending on traffic.

How many days do I need in Cairo?

Three to four days covers the Giza Pyramids, the Grand Egyptian Museum, Islamic Cairo and Khan el-Khalili without rushing.

Can I visit the Pyramids without a tour?

Yes, you can buy a ticket at the gate. A licensed Egyptologist guide helps you avoid touts and explains what you are looking at.
